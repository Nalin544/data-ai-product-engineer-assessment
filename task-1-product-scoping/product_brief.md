# Marketing Performance Insight Hub

# Problem Statement

Marketing teams currently rely on manually collecting and combining data from multiple platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and Google Analytics to understand campaign performance.

This process is slow, inconsistent, and highly dependent on specific team members. Different analysts may interpret and present the same data differently, leading to inconsistent reporting and delays in decision-making.

As the number of campaigns and channels increases, the current workflow becomes difficult to scale and inefficient for both internal teams and stakeholders.

The goal of this product is to create a centralized internal tool that standardizes marketing performance reporting, reduces manual effort, and helps teams quickly identify which marketing channels and campaigns are performing well and where improvements are needed.

# Target Users

    ## Primary Users
        Internal marketing analysts who are responsible for monitoring campaign performance, preparing reports, and identifying optimization opportunities.

    ## Secondary Users
        Account managers and marketing stakeholders who need quick visibility into campaign performance across multiple channels.

The first version of the product is designed mainly for internal teams instead of clients. Internal users already understand the context behind campaign metrics and can help validate the accuracy and usefulness of the system before expanding access externally.

# Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an internal marketing performance dashboard that consolidates campaign data from multiple marketing platforms into a single standardized interface.

The platform will automatically collect marketing metrics from connected sources and present them in a unified format. Users will be able to compare performance across channels, monitor trends, and identify high-performing or underperforming campaigns without manually combining data from different systems.

The tool will also provide simple automated insights such as:
- highest performing channel by ROAS
- campaigns with declining CTR
- channels with high CPA
- week-over-week performance trends

The objective is to reduce reporting time, improve consistency, and help marketing teams make faster data-driven decisions.

# V1 Scope

The first version of the platform will focus on solving the core reporting problem with a simple and reliable workflow.

Included in V1:
- Integration with major marketing platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and Google Analytics
- Standardized dashboard for campaign performance tracking
- Key marketing metrics including:
  - impressions
  - clicks
  - CTR
  - conversions
  - CPA
  - ROAS
- Channel comparison view
- Date range filtering
- Export functionality for CSV or PDF summaries
- Basic automated insights and trend summaries

The primary goal of V1 is to reduce manual reporting effort and improve consistency across the team.

# Out of Scope

To keep the first version focused and practical, several features are intentionally excluded from V1.

Excluded from V1:
- Real-time analytics and streaming updates
- Predictive AI or forecasting models
- Client-facing portal and authentication system
- Budget recommendation engine
- Advanced attribution modeling
- Custom dashboard builders

These features add significant technical complexity and require validated historical data and user feedback before implementation.

# Architecture

Marketing Platforms APIs
        ↓
Data Ingestion Layer
        ↓
BigQuery Data Warehouse
        ↓
Transformation + Insight Engine
        ↓
Dashboard/UI

The system collects campaign data from multiple marketing platforms through APIs. The data is processed and stored in BigQuery, where transformations and insight generation are performed before displaying results in the dashboard interface.

# User Flow

1. User opens the dashboard
2. User selects a date range or marketing channel
3. System retrieves and processes campaign data
4. Dashboard displays standardized metrics and insights
5. User compares performance across channels and campaigns
6. User exports or shares findings with stakeholders

# Success Metrics

The product will be considered successful if:
- reporting time is reduced significantly
- analysts can answer performance questions faster
- reporting becomes standardized across the organization
- dependency on specific employees decreases
- stakeholders trust the insights generated by the platform
